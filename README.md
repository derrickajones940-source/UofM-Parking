# UofM Parking Survival App

## Concept Brief

The UofM Parking Survival App is designed to help University of Memphis students and visitors find the best available parking option based on parking availability, cost, parking restrictions, and walking distance. The app will allow users to see whether parking lots are available, getting full, or full, and can provide notifications when a preferred parking lot is becoming full. Users may also be able to report parking conditions so that other students can see recent parking information. The goal is to make finding parking easier, reduce the time spent searching for parking, and help users avoid parking restrictions and unnecessary parking tickets.
